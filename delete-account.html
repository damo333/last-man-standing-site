Delete your Last Man Standing account

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open Last Man Standing and sign in.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

If you can’t use the app, email damo3550@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, asking for your account to be deleted. We’ll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What’s deleted

  • Your sign-in (email and password)
  • Your display name and favourite team
  • Your league memberships, picks, results and buy-back requests
  • Your payment status in leagues (whether an organiser marked you as paid)
  • Your device’s push notification token and any notifications waiting to be sent

Nothing is kept afterwards. If you organise a league, it passes to one of its admins, or to the player who joined first. If you’re the only player in it, the league is deleted too.
