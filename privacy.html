Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy explains what the Last Man Standing app (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: damo3550@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider.
  • Profile: the display name and favourite team you choose.
  • Game data: the leagues you create or join, your picks, results and status, and any buy-back requests.
  • League payments: whether a league organiser has marked you as having paid an entry fee. The app never handles money. Payment links open services like Revolut or PayPal, whose own privacy policies apply.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a push token for your device and its platform (Android or iOS), so we can send you reminders and results.

We don’t collect your location, contacts or photos. There are no ads, analytics or tracking, and we never sell your data.

How it’s used

Only to run the game: signing you in, running your leagues, showing standings, and sending the emails and notifications you’d expect (sign-up confirmation, pick reminders, results, buy-back decisions and payment reminders). We rely on providing the service you signed up for (contract) as the legal basis.

Who can see what

  • Players in your leagues see your display name, favourite team, status, and your picks once each gameweek’s deadline has passed.
  • League organisers and admins also see whether you’ve been marked as paid.
  • Your email address isn’t shown to other players.

Services we use

  • Supabase: hosts the database and handles sign-in.
  • Expo, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service: deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Our email provider: sends sign-up and account emails.
  • football-data.org: supplies fixtures and results. No personal data is sent to it.

These providers process data only on our behalf. Some may process it outside the European Economic Area under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your data is deleted straight away. League history that other players see (such as past results) no longer shows you.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, or ask for a copy of it. You can change your name and favourite team in the app, and delete your account from your profile. For anything else, email us. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to your data protection authority. In Ireland, that’s the Data Protection Commission.

Children

The app isn’t intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we’ll tell you in the app.
